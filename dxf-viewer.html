Inspect structured DXF entities, not a screenshot

AGBoard reads ASCII DXF layers and common 2D entities including lines, arcs, circles, polylines, ellipses, splines, blocks, text and hatches.

Layer names remain available in the layer panel, where each layer can be shown, hidden, reordered, locked or recolored.

What AGBoard reads

Curves and paths

Lines, arcs, circles, polylines, lightweight polylines, ellipses and spline geometry.

Reusable content

Blocks and inserted geometry are expanded into the interactive drawing view.

Drawing context

Layer organization, text and common filled hatch geometry remain visible for inspection.

What you can do

  • Toggle, reorder, lock and recolor DXF layers
  • Measure distances, offsets, angles and clearances
  • Select, move, duplicate, resize and delete compatible shapes
  • Rotate, mirror and panelize selected geometry
  • Export a high-resolution PNG or vector PDF
  • Save compatible drawing geometry back to ASCII DXF

Inspect on iPhone and iPad

The same touch-first tools stay available from a phone or a larger iPad workspace.

Frequently asked questions

Does AGBoard open binary DXF files?

AGBoard's DXF support is for ASCII DXF drawings. Convert a binary DXF to ASCII before opening it.

Are DXF layers preserved?

Yes. Parsed layer names and their geometry are available in the layer panel.

Can I edit and export the drawing?

Compatible shapes can be transformed or edited and saved to DXF; PNG, PDF and CSV outputs are also available where applicable.

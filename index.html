Designed for real PCB inspection

  • Multiple file formats.
    Gerber RS-274X/X2/X3 (inner layers included), Barco / Ucamco DPF layers (.dpf), KiCad boards and schematics (.kicad_pcb, .kicad_sch, .kicad_pro projects, .kicad_mod footprints), Altium boards and footprint libraries (.PcbDoc, .PcbLib, Circuit Studio .CSPcbDoc), Excellon drill files (Altium, Allegro and Zuken variants included), AutoCAD DXF drawings (ASCII or binary), ODB++ jobs (panels expanded), IPC-2581 designs, Eagle and Landrex BoardView .brd boards, Gerber job files (.gbrjob), IPC-D-356 netlists and CSV/TXT/POS component placements.
  • Interactive 3D view.
    Switch the board into 3D and rotate, zoom and inspect it: a substrate as thick as the file says — 1.6 mm when it is silent — with its routed edge, stacked copper layers, drill holes and placed components. Flip it over to inspect the bottom side, and select the substrate or a component directly in the 3D view.
  • Layer control.
    Show, hide, reorder, lock, recolor, duplicate and export layers from a touch-friendly panel.
  • Editing tools.
    Move, duplicate, resize and delete shapes, including rounded rectangles and editable corner radius where supported. Width, height and angle are the shape's own, and a shape that is really several — an ODB++ user symbol holding six openings — can be taken apart, edited piece by piece and put back as one object.
  • Transform workflows.
    Mirror, rotate, reset and panelize layers, or apply transformations to a multi-selection group.
  • Measurement tools.
    Measure edge-to-edge, center-to-center or between any two points. Read distance, dx/dy, angle, clearance or overlap, then review the history or export it as CSV.
  • Stencil area ratio.
    Screen a solder-paste stencil against IPC-7525C: every opening's area and aspect ratios. Openings under the limits are listed and highlighted on the board, and the report exports as CSV or PDF. You choose the stencil layer when the files do not say which it is, state the foil thickness — no PCB file carries it — and draw step zones straight over the board. Text, legend and fiducials the files declare are left out, any other artwork can be left out with a tap, and your settings stay while the board is open.
  • Component search.
    Components come straight from the file: Gerber X3 component layers (reference, value, package, mount type and rotation), KiCad, Altium, ODB++, Eagle and BoardView boards — no placement file needed. You can still import a CSV/TXT/POS with parser profiles. Search by reference, value or package, and highlight top or bottom components.
  • Net trace & search.
    Tap copper to trace physically connected geometry without a netlist. In Landrex BoardView files, tap a pad to highlight every pad and test point on its named net, with top/bottom separation. Net search is also available from IPC-D-356, ODB++ and IPC-2581.
  • Markup & annotations.
    Add notes, arrows, rectangles and ellipses right on the board. They stay pinned through zoom and pan and are saved with the project — ideal for review and field inspection.
  • Save in the original format, or convert.
    Write a board back as ODB++, DXF, IPC-2581, Gerber or a placement file — or convert it into a format it did not come from: a Gerber set, a KiCad, Altium, Eagle or BoardView design can leave as ODB++, IPC-2581 or DXF. Export Gerber and drill together with the .gbrjob manifest, the IPC-D-356 netlist and the placement CSV. A KiCad or Eagle board is never regenerated from what is on screen: the original file is kept, an untouched board comes back byte for byte, and a moved or deleted element is rewritten in place — anything the format cannot express is declined with the reason rather than approximated — and where a file is still possible, you can choose to export anyway, with every omission named.
  • Fast workflow.
    Recent files on a long-press of Open, drag & drop from the Files app, and board statistics (size, hole table, smallest drill and trace).
  • Board previews in Files.
    With AGBoard Pro, the Files app shows the board itself: a thumbnail of every Gerber, drill, KiCad, Altium, Eagle, DXF or IPC-2581 file, and a layer-by-layer PDF preview when you tap it — drawn on your device.

Supported formats

FormatExtensionsWhat is read
Gerber.gbr .gtl .gbl .gto .gts .gko .gm1…RS-274X, X2 and X3. Inner layers, macros, regions, step-and-repeat. X2 attributes set each layer's role; X3 adds components (reference, value, package, mount type, rotation) and net names. Zuken photoplot sets are recognised through their .phl index, each .phd film on the side of the board it belongs to; Zuken CR-8000 sets through their .fpl photo list. Files with unusual extensions are recognised by their content. A whole set opens from a folder or a .zip.
Barco DPF (UCAM).dpfBarco / Ucamco Dynamic Process Format: PCB layers with pads, tracks, arcs, contours, complex apertures, blocks, polarities and nets when present. Separate source objects remain individually selectable. Export edits as Gerber .gbr; edited DPF files are not regenerated in their native format.
KiCad.kicad_pcb .kicad_sch .kicad_pro .kicad_mod .kicad_sym .kicad_wksThe native KiCad board, opened directly. Layers, tracks, arcs, vias, pads, copper zones, silkscreen, components and nets. Schematics too: symbols, wires, junctions, net labels and hierarchical sheets, with wires carrying the same net names as the board. A project opens the board beside it; a footprint opens on its own. A symbol library opens as a contact sheet and a drawing sheet as the project's title block. Boards from KiCad 5 to the current version; schematics from KiCad 6 on.
Altium Designer.PcbDoc .PcbLib .CSPcbDocAltium's own board and footprint library, opened directly. Tracks, arcs, pads, vias, copper pours, dimensions, silkscreen, board outline, components and nets. Unicode silkscreen included. Circuit Studio boards open the same way.
Excellon drill.drl .exc .nc .xln .tap .xnc…Plated and non-plated holes, slots, arcs and repeated patterns, with the hole table in the board statistics. Altium's legacy binary .DRL and blind-via .TX1 files, Allegro's nc_param.txt and Zuken CR-8000 drill files are read too.
ODB++folder · .zip .7z .tgz .tar .odb…Complete multi-layer job; archives are extracted automatically, panels expanded, nets and components included.
IPC-2581.cvg .xmlLayers, pads, traces, nets and components in a single file.
AutoCAD DXF.dxfASCII or binary drawings: layers, lines, arcs, circles, polylines, ellipses, splines, blocks, text and hatches.
Eagle / Fusion.brdEagle board (XML): tracks, pads, vias, outline, silkscreen, text.
Landrex BoardView.brd .brd2Inspection data: outline, component envelopes, pins, test points and named nets.
Gerber job.gbrjobOpens a whole referenced Gerber set in one tap, each layer with its exact role.
IPC-D-356 netlist.ipc .356 .d356Electrical net list, enabling net search and highlighting.
Component placement.csv .txt .pos .xy…Pick-and-place files with reusable parser profiles for the column layout.
AGBoard project.agprojThe layers and their settings, as saved with Export project, reopened as they were.

Not read: Altium schematics (.SchDoc), KiCad 5 and Eagle schematics (.sch), Cadence Allegro databases (.brd), RAR and password-protected archives. For a board, export Gerber, ODB++ or IPC-2581 from the design tool; a KiCad 5 schematic opens once KiCad 6 or later has saved it.

Everything is parsed on your device — no upload, no account.

Frequently asked questions

Which file formats are supported?

Gerber RS-274X/X2/X3 (inner layers included), Barco / Ucamco DPF layers (.dpf), KiCad and Altium boards and footprint libraries (Circuit Studio included), Excellon drill files, AutoCAD DXF drawings (ASCII or binary), ODB++ jobs with panels expanded, IPC-2581 designs, Eagle and Landrex BoardView .brd boards, Gerber job files (.gbrjob), IPC-D-356 netlists and CSV/TXT/POS component placements.

Can I see my boards in the Files app?

Yes, with AGBoard Pro. The Files app then shows a picture of the board as the thumbnail of each Gerber, drill, KiCad, Altium, Eagle, DXF or IPC-2581 file, and a layer-by-layer PDF preview when you tap it. Both are drawn on your device. Open the app once after subscribing.

Are my design files private?

Yes. Every file is parsed and rendered on your device - nothing is uploaded, no AGBoard account is required and the app contains no tracking.

Is the app free?

AGBoard is free to download and lets you open five files at no charge. After those five file opens, an auto-renewable monthly or annual AGBoard Pro subscription is required to continue using the app. Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled in your Apple Account settings.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad running iOS 17 or later. Rendering is GPU-accelerated with Metal; performance still depends on the device and the size and complexity of the board.

Is a Mac or Windows version planned?

Yes — Mac and Windows versions are planned for February 2027.

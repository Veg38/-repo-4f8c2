Designed for real PCB inspection

  • Multiple file formats.
    Gerber RS-274X/X2/X3 (inner layers included), KiCad boards (.kicad_pcb, .kicad_pro projects, .kicad_mod footprints), Altium boards and footprint libraries (.PcbDoc, .PcbLib), Excellon drill files, AutoCAD ASCII DXF drawings, ODB++ jobs (panels expanded), IPC-2581 designs, Eagle and Landrex BoardView .brd boards, Gerber job files (.gbrjob), IPC-D-356 netlists and CSV/TXT/POS component placements.
  • Interactive 3D view.
    Switch the board into 3D and rotate, zoom and inspect it: a 1.6 mm substrate with its routed edge, stacked copper layers, drill holes and placed components. Flip it over to inspect the bottom side, and select the substrate or a component directly in the 3D view.
  • Layer control.
    Show, hide, reorder, lock, recolor, duplicate and export layers from a touch-friendly panel.
  • Editing tools.
    Move, duplicate, resize and delete shapes, including rounded rectangles and editable corner radius where supported.
  • Transform workflows.
    Mirror, rotate, reset and panelize layers, or apply transformations to a multi-selection group.
  • Measurement tools.
    Measure edge-to-edge, center-to-center or between any two points. Read distance, dx/dy, angle, clearance or overlap, then review the history or export it as CSV.
  • Component search.
    Components come straight from the file: Gerber X3 component layers (reference, value, package, mount type and rotation), KiCad, Altium, ODB++, Eagle and BoardView boards — no placement file needed. You can still import a CSV/TXT/POS with parser profiles. Search by reference, value or package, and highlight top or bottom components.
  • Net trace & search.
    Tap copper to trace physically connected geometry without a netlist. In Landrex BoardView files, tap a pad to highlight every pad and test point on its named net, with top/bottom separation. Net search is also available from IPC-D-356, ODB++ and IPC-2581.
  • Markup & annotations.
    Add notes, arrows, rectangles and ellipses right on the board. They stay pinned through zoom and pan and are saved with the project — ideal for review and field inspection.
  • Save in the original format.
    Write a board back as ODB++, DXF, IPC-2581, Gerber or a placement file. Export Gerber and drill together with the .gbrjob manifest, the IPC-D-356 netlist and the placement CSV. A KiCad or Eagle board is never regenerated from what is on screen: the original file is kept, an untouched board comes back byte for byte, and a moved or deleted element is rewritten in place — anything the format cannot express is declined with the reason rather than approximated.
  • Fast workflow.
    Recent files on a long-press of Open, drag & drop from the Files app, and board statistics (size, hole table, smallest drill and trace).

Supported formats

FormatExtensionsWhat is read
Gerber.gbr .gtl .gbl .gto .gts .gko .gm1…RS-274X, X2 and X3. Inner layers, macros, regions, step-and-repeat. X2 attributes set each layer's role; X3 adds components (reference, value, package, mount type, rotation) and net names.
KiCad.kicad_pcb .kicad_pro .kicad_modThe native KiCad board, opened directly. Layers, tracks, arcs, vias, pads, copper zones, silkscreen, components and nets. A project opens the board beside it; a footprint opens on its own. KiCad 5 to current.
Altium Designer.PcbDoc .PcbLibAltium's own board and footprint library, opened directly. Tracks, arcs, pads, vias, copper pours, dimensions, silkscreen, board outline, components and nets. Unicode silkscreen included.
Excellon drill.drl .exc .nc .xln .tap…Plated and non-plated holes, with the hole table in the board statistics.
ODB++folder · .zip .tgz .tarComplete multi-layer job; archives are extracted automatically, panels expanded, nets and components included.
IPC-2581.cvg .xmlLayers, pads, traces, nets and components in a single file.
AutoCAD DXF.dxfASCII drawings: layers, lines, arcs, circles, polylines, ellipses, splines, blocks, text and hatches.
Eagle / Fusion.brdEagle board (XML): tracks, pads, vias, outline, silkscreen, text.
Landrex BoardView.brdInspection data: outline, component envelopes, pins, test points and named nets.
Gerber job.gbrjobOpens a whole referenced Gerber set in one tap, each layer with its exact role.
IPC-D-356 netlist.ipc .356 .d356Electrical net list, enabling net search and highlighting.
Component placement.csv .txt .pos .xy…Pick-and-place files with reusable parser profiles for the column layout.

On both iPhone and iPad, open files from the Files app or iCloud — a single file or a whole fabrication archive, every layer at once. On iPad you can also drag & drop them straight onto the canvas.

Inspect

Share HD PNG images, multi-page vector PDFs, CSV reports — or a project file your team can reopen as-is.

Frequently asked questions

Which file formats are supported?

Gerber RS-274X/X2/X3 (inner layers included), KiCad and Altium boards and footprint libraries, Excellon drill files, AutoCAD ASCII DXF drawings, ODB++ jobs with panels expanded, IPC-2581 designs, Eagle and Landrex BoardView .brd boards, Gerber job files (.gbrjob), IPC-D-356 netlists and CSV/TXT/POS component placements.

Are my design files private?

Yes. Every file is parsed and rendered on your device - nothing is uploaded, no AGBoard account is required and the app contains no tracking.

Is the app free?

AGBoard is free to download and lets you open five files at no charge. After those five file opens, an auto-renewable monthly or annual AGBoard Pro subscription is required to continue using the app. Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled in your Apple Account settings.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad running iOS 17 or later. Rendering is GPU-accelerated with Metal; performance still depends on the device and the size and complexity of the board.

Is a Mac or Windows version planned?

Yes — Mac and Windows versions are planned for February 2027.

Mobile PCB inspection

Take a professional PCB workspace anywhere

Open a fabrication file at the bench, beside a machine or in the field. AGBoard brings precise PCB inspection and editing to the device already in your hand.

Watch the video

A PCB viewer built around touch

Pinch into dense pads, move across large boards and isolate the layers you need without turning a mobile screen into a reduced desktop interface.

Files open from the Files app, iCloud Drive and compatible document providers. Parsing, rendering and editing remain on the device.

What you can inspect

Complete files

Open Gerber, Excellon, ODB++, IPC-2581, KiCad, Altium, DXF, Eagle, BoardView and placement data.

Fast inspection

Control layers, use realistic colours, search components and nets, and inspect the reconstructed board in 3D.

Useful outputs

Save supported source formats or create PNG, vector PDF, CSV reports, printouts and shareable AGBoard projects.

What you can do

  • Open one layer or a complete multi-file fabrication set
  • Measure between pads, shapes or arbitrary board points
  • Highlight physically connected copper or a named logical net
  • Review top and bottom components by reference, value or package
  • Add notes, arrows and review shapes that stay attached to the board
  • Work without uploading confidential PCB data

Frequently asked questions

Can I open files from iCloud Drive?

Yes. AGBoard uses the system document picker, so iCloud Drive, On My iPhone or iPad and compatible Files providers remain available.

Does the app need an AGBoard account?

No. There is no AGBoard account and PCB files are processed locally on the device.

Can a phone handle a multi-layer board?

Yes. The interface is designed for touch navigation and Metal-accelerated rendering on supported iPhone and iPad devices.

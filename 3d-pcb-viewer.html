Interactive 3D inspection

Turn manufacturing data into a board you can inspect

Move from precise 2D layers to an interactive 3D board. Rotate, zoom and flip the assembly to understand its outline, holes, copper stack and component placement.

Watch the video

3D that remains connected to the PCB data

AGBoard reconstructs the substrate from the board profile and keeps routed edges and drill holes visible. Copper and available component placement data are positioned against the same board coordinates.

Select the substrate or a component directly in the scene, inspect the bottom side and return to the corresponding 2D data whenever more detail is needed.

What you can inspect

Real profile

The substrate follows the available board outline instead of using a generic rectangle.

Layer stack

Copper layers and holes retain their relative position through the reconstructed board thickness.

Placed parts

Components are shown when supported source data provides their package, side, position and rotation.

What you can do

  • Rotate and zoom the complete PCB with touch gestures
  • Flip to the bottom side without losing board orientation
  • Inspect routed edges, cutouts and drilled holes
  • Select a component directly from the 3D scene
  • Compare component placement with copper and silkscreen
  • Return to 2D layers for measurements and editing

Frequently asked questions

Which files can produce a 3D board?

AGBoard builds 3D from supported board geometry. Component detail depends on the placement and package information present in the source file.

Can I inspect the bottom of the PCB?

Yes. Rotate or flip the board to inspect bottom copper and bottom-side components.

Is the 3D model uploaded to a server?

No. Reconstruction and rendering are performed on the device.

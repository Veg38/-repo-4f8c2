Ask about a format

A mobile workspace for real Gerber data

AGBoard reads Gerber RS-274X as well as X2 layer and object attributes. X3 component data can provide references, values, packages, mount types, rotations and net names directly from the Gerber set.

Open individual layers, a Gerber job file or a fabrication archive. Inner copper, solder mask, silkscreen, paste, profile and drill data stay separated so the board remains easy to inspect.

What AGBoard reads

Geometry

Aperture flashes, stroked traces, arcs, regions, cutouts, aperture macros and step-and-repeat data.

Layer identity

X2 file attributes identify copper, mask, silkscreen, profile and other manufacturing roles.

X3 intelligence

Search component references, packages and nets when the Gerber set contains X3 assembly data.

What you can do

  • Show, hide, reorder, lock and recolor every layer
  • Measure pad spacing, clearances, angles and board dimensions
  • Trace physically connected copper without a separate netlist
  • Move, duplicate, resize, rotate and panelize editable geometry
  • Inspect the reconstructed board in an interactive 3D view
  • Export Gerber, Excellon drill, .gbrjob, PNG, PDF and CSV results

Frequently asked questions

Can AGBoard open a complete Gerber set?

Yes. Open a .gbrjob file, a fabrication archive or the related layers together to build the complete board stack.

Are Gerber X2 and X3 attributes supported?

Yes. X2 attributes classify layers and objects; X3 data can add components and net names when present in the files.

Do my Gerber files leave the device?

No. Gerber parsing, rendering, editing and export are performed on your iPhone or iPad.

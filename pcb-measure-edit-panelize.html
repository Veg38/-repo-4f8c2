Measurement and editing

Measure, adjust and panelize PCB geometry with touch

Go beyond passive viewing. Measure real board relationships, adjust supported shapes with numeric dimensions and build repeated production layouts from the same mobile workspace.

Watch the video

Precise operations with visible results

Choose Edges, Centers or Points, then select the two references to measure. AGBoard displays the dimension directly on the board with distance, horizontal and vertical offsets, angle, true clearance or overlap.

Editing tools operate on supported source geometry. Move, duplicate, resize or delete selected shapes, apply group transforms and preview panelization before committing it.

What you can inspect

Measurements

Measure edge-to-edge, center-to-center or point-to-point, snap to objects or the grid, review the history and export the results as CSV.

Shape editing

Adjust supported dimensions and corner radius with a live preview before applying the change.

Panelization

Choose rows, columns and pitch to duplicate selected board geometry into a regular panel.

What you can do

  • Measure the true edge-to-edge clearance between two pads or shapes
  • Switch between edge, center and free-point dimensions
  • Read dx, dy, angle, overlap and the measured witness points
  • Move, duplicate, resize or delete supported shapes
  • Rotate, mirror or reset a layer or multi-selection
  • Preview rows, columns and spacing before panelization
  • Export measurements, reports, images and printable PDF

Frequently asked questions

Can AGBoard measure actual clearances?

Yes. Edge mode places the dimension on the nearest physical edges and reports clearance or overlap. Center and point modes remain available for placement and mechanical dimensions.

Does panelization change the original immediately?

No. The panel setup provides a preview and is applied only after confirmation.

Can every imported property be edited?

Editing depends on what the source format can represent safely. AGBoard keeps unsupported operations explicit instead of silently approximating board data.

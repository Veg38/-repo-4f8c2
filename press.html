Why AGBoard is useful

PCB inspection leaves the workstation

Engineers and technicians often need fabrication data at a repair bench, beside production equipment or during field inspection. AGBoard keeps layers, measurements, components and nets available on the mobile device already at hand.

Manufacturing data stays structured

Gerber X2/X3, ODB++, IPC-2581, KiCad, Altium and BoardView carry different combinations of geometry, assembly and connectivity data. AGBoard reconstructs that information instead of presenting only a flattened image.

Confidential boards remain on the device

Parsing, rendering, editing and export happen locally. PCB files are not sent to an AGBoard server and no AGBoard account is required.

What the current release does

  • Opens Gerber, GBX, Excellon, ODB++, IPC-2581, KiCad, Altium, DXF, Eagle, BoardView, IPC-D-356 and placement files
  • Controls and inspects complete PCB layer stacks
  • Searches available components, packages, pins, test points and named nets
  • Measures between pads, shapes or arbitrary board points
  • Adds review annotations and edits supported source geometry
  • Panelizes selected board geometry with a live preview
  • Reconstructs the board profile, holes, copper and available components in interactive 3D
  • Exports supported source formats, PNG, vector PDF, CSV reports and print output

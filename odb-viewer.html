Keep the ODB++ job structure visible

AGBoard reads the ODB++ matrix, steps, layers, features, component data and EDA net information. Panel steps are expanded into the board view while their source data remains organized by layer.

Large jobs open with progress feedback and use the same accelerated canvas as Gerber projects, making dense production data practical to inspect on an iPhone or iPad.

What AGBoard reads

Layer stack

Copper, mask, silkscreen, paste, drill, route, document and board-outline roles from the job matrix.

Panels and geometry

Step repetitions, lines, arcs, pads, surfaces, routed profiles, cutouts and drill features.

Assembly data

Component references, packages, pins, top or bottom placement and logical net relationships.

What you can do

  • Search components by reference, value or package
  • Search and highlight named nets and their connected features
  • Inspect individual manufacturing layers or the combined board
  • Measure pads, holes, distances, clearances and dimensions
  • Explore the substrate, copper, drills and components in 3D
  • Save compatible edits back to ODB++ and export visual or CSV reports

Frequently asked questions

Which ODB++ packages can I open?

AGBoard accepts an ODB++ directory and common .zip, .tgz, .tar and .tar.gz archives.

Are ODB++ panels, components and nets available?

Yes. Panel steps are expanded, while supported component, package, pin and EDA net data power search and highlighting.

Is the ODB++ archive uploaded?

No. Extraction, parsing and rendering happen locally on the device.

AGBoard brings professional PCB inspection and editing to iPhone and iPad

AGBoard - PCBEditor is now available on the App Store, giving electronics
engineers, repair technicians and PCB professionals a touch-first way to open,
inspect and edit board data away from a desktop workstation.

The app reads manufacturing and design formats including Gerber X2 and X3,
Excellon, ODB++, IPC-2581, KiCad, Altium, DXF, Eagle, Landrex BoardView,
IPC-D-356 and component placement files. Users can inspect individual layers,
search components and named nets, measure between pads or shapes, add review
annotations, edit supported geometry, panelize a board and export professional
images, PDF and CSV reports.

An interactive 3D view reconstructs the board profile, thickness, copper,
drilled holes and available placed components. The board can be rotated, zoomed
and flipped to inspect both sides.

PCB files are parsed and rendered locally on the device. They are not uploaded
to an AGBoard server, and no AGBoard account is required. This makes the app
suitable for confidential designs used at the bench, beside production
equipment or during field inspection.

AGBoard is available for iPhone and iPad running iOS or iPadOS 17 or later. The
download includes five file openings before an AGBoard Pro monthly or annual
subscription is required.

AGBOARD - PCBEDITOR: FACT SHEET

Product
AGBoard - PCBEditor

Category
Professional PCB viewer and editor

Availability
Apple App Store for iPhone and iPad
https://apps.apple.com/app/id6784736690

System requirement
iOS 17 or later / iPadOS 17 or later

Languages
English and French

Access model
Free download with five file openings included. AGBoard Pro monthly or annual
subscription required afterwards.

Primary audiences
Electronics engineers, PCB designers, repair technicians, manufacturing teams,
quality inspectors, field technicians and electronics students.

Principal formats
Gerber RS-274X/X2/X3, Gerber job, GBX, Excellon, ODB++, IPC-2581, IPC-D-356,
KiCad, Altium, AutoCAD ASCII DXF, Eagle, Landrex BoardView and component
placement files.

Key capabilities
Layer inspection and control; interactive 3D view; component and net search;
physical copper tracing; pad-to-pad and shape-to-shape measurements; annotations;
supported geometry editing; layer transformations; panelization; source-format
save where supported; PNG, vector PDF, CSV and print exports.

Privacy
PCB parsing and rendering are performed locally. Board files are not uploaded
to an AGBoard server. No AGBoard account is required.
